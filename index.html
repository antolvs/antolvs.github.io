BRANESCAN

This is my personal website/blog. You'll find things I'm working on, have worked on, and more!

I have a lot of hobbies; I'm always interested in all-things-creativity. I enjoy creating experiences whether it's through games, music, videos, or art.

Some quick facts:
  • I'm a cyber security graduate from The University of Texas at San Antonio.
  • I also hold an associates degree in business administration.
  • My psuedonym, branescan, is a theoretical physics term relating to the search of higher dimensions. I took it from my favorite video game, Half-Life 2.
  • My favorite movie is Tron: Legacy.
  • I love Star Wars and run a TikTok account dedicated to it. It's mostly nostalgia bait.
  • I collect comic books, mostly trades, but a few slabs and single issues as well. You can see my collection here.
  • I love music. Every genre. Yes. Every genre. Follow my Spotify and check out my record collection.
  • I created the world-famous McDonald's ambience used in every skit/meme
